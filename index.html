Questions you might have.

Who are you?

A small web design team founded by Yash, a UX designer with more than 4 years of experience designing websites and apps. We're new as a studio, which is why the price is low.

Who will I talk to?

Yash, our founder, leads every project. You have one point of contact from the first email to the handover.

How can it be this fast?

The 7-day offer covers one kind of website: a small site for a local business. That keeps the plan simple. We also use AI tools for the routine building work, so our time goes into the design.

Is 7 days guaranteed?

It's our target for a site of up to 5 pages, counted from the day we have your logo, photos and text. We confirm your delivery date by email before you pay. A shop, bookings or logins take longer, and we tell you how long first.

Do you only design, or build it too?

Both. We design it and develop it, front end and back end. Contact forms, bookings, logins or a shop are built by us as well. Bigger features are quoted separately.

Will I be top of Google?

Not from the launch alone. We set up basic SEO when we build your site: page titles, descriptions, Google Maps and the setup Google needs to list you. Getting to the top takes months of ongoing work after launch, like new content and local listings. That is a monthly service, and we don't offer it.

What if I don't like it?

You see the full design before you pay the second half, and 2 rounds of changes are included.

How do I pay?

We send an invoice. You pay half before we start and half when you approve the finished site.

Who looks after the site later?

You do. We hand everything over with a short video that shows how to change the text and photos.

Where are you based?

In India. We work with business owners abroad by email and video call.
