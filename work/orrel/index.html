House, Douro valley, 2025

Casa Ribeira

A low house of concrete and granite, set into an old vineyard terrace above the river.

The site was a narrow terrace held up by a granite wall two hundred years old. We kept the wall and pushed the house into the slope behind it, so from the river you see the old stone and one long window, nothing else.

Inside, the ceilings are concrete cast against rough boards, the floors are oak, and every room looks down the valley. A stair of solid concrete climbs to a skylight and a roof terrace among the olive trees.

Library, Aveiro estuary, 2024

The Salt Store

A public library inside a 19th-century salt warehouse, under its original timber roof.

For a hundred years the building held salt from the pans outside. We cleaned the stone, repaired the timber trusses one by one and left the white salt marks on the lower walls.

The new work is all pale timber: shelves that stand clear of the old walls, long oak tables and two tall doors that fold open to the water in summer.

Winery, Douro valley, 2023

Socalco cellar

A wine cellar dug into a vineyard hillside, lit by one slot of daylight.

From the road you see a single concrete opening in the terraces, and vines growing over the roof. Everything else is underground, where the temperature stays the same all year without a machine.

The barrel hall is one long concrete vault. A narrow slot along its top lets in a line of daylight that moves across the floor as the day goes on.

Public baths, Atlantic coast, 2022

Pedra Alta sea baths

A sea pool on the rocks with a low concrete pavilion, open every summer since 2022.

The tide fills the pool twice a day. We placed it where the rocks already made a basin, so the new concrete wall is short and the sea does most of the work.

The pavilion holds the changing rooms, showers and a small café. It is low enough to see over from the road, and its slatted timber cabins smell of salt by August.

Housing, Porto, 2026

Rua Nova flats

Fourteen flats around a shared courtyard and an old plane tree, on a narrow street in the city.

The plot was a car park behind a row of tiled houses, with one plane tree in the middle. We built around the tree. Every flat opens onto the courtyard by a wide gallery that works as a front porch.

On the street side the block is lime-washed, with deep window reveals that keep the sun off in summer. Building started in spring and the first families move in next year.
