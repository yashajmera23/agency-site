House rules & questions

What’s the door policy?

Over 21, with a valid ID. Come to dance, be kind, and you’re in. We say no to big groups that turn up drunk.

Can I take photos?

Not on the floor. We put a sticker on your camera at the door. The Yard and the bar are fine.

Is there a dress code?

No. Wear what you can dance in for six hours. Black is common, not required.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes, until 03:00, with your wristband.

Lost something?

Email lost@sublevel.example within a week. We keep things for 30 days.
