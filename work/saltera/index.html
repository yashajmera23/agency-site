Saltera is four houses of pale stone, set far apart along a kilometre of cliff. Each one has its own pool, its own path to the water and a host who knows when to leave you alone.

The houses were built with the cliff, not on top of it. Walls are the same limestone you swim off, roofs are planted with wild thyme, and every room looks at the sea.

There is no lobby and no restaurant full of strangers. Breakfast comes to your terrace, dinner is wherever you want it, and the only other guests are the ones you brought.

The villas

From a house for two couples to one for a whole wedding party. Each comes with its own staff, chef on request and the boat for a day.

A kilometre of cliff,
and nobody else on it

Capo Salterno is a headland of limestone, pine and wild herbs, with coves below that you reach on foot or by boat. The nearest village is twenty minutes away, and it has one bakery and two bars.

Getting here

Fifty minutes by car from the airport, or an hour by boat from the harbour. We collect you either way, and the car waits while you swim if you want to stop on the way.

Days on the water and off it

  • The day boat

    A wooden boat and a skipper, out to coves you can only reach from the water. Lunch on board, back by five.

  • The long table

    Our chef cooks what came in on the boats that morning, served on your terrace or on the cliff above the cove.

  • The stone spa

    A warm pool in a room cut from the rock, lit by one slot of daylight. Your host books treatments for you.

  • Cliff swims

    Flat rocks, deep clear water and a ladder back up. A guide shows you the best places to jump on your first morning.

Inside the houses

Linen, olive wood and the same pale stone as the cliff. Furniture by makers from the island, and nothing on the walls that competes with the window.

The small things

A key you can feel, a towel that dries in the sun, a card written by hand. The name sits on them quietly, the way it does on the stone by the path.
