Our story

Two ovens, one wharf, since 2011.

Hanna and Theo Lund took over the old net loft at the end of Wharf Street with one deck oven and a sourdough starter from Theo’s grandmother in Sweden. The fishermen were the first regulars. They still are.

We bake with flour milled in Maine, butter from a dairy up the river, and whatever blueberries the summer gives us.
