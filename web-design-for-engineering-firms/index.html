What clients check before they shortlist you.

  1. Have you done a job like mine?

    A project list with photos, the type of client and your role answers this faster than any proposal.

  2. Who are your engineers?

    Licences, years of experience and areas of practice. People who buy engineering services look for this before they call.

  3. What exactly do you do?

    Civil, structural, MEP, environmental. A page for each service helps the right clients, and the right Google searches, find you.

  4. Are you still busy?

    An old copyright year, an empty news page or a COVID notice makes a firm look closed. Good engineers looking for a job notice it too.

Questions you might have.

Can you show our projects without naming clients?

Yes. Many firms describe a project by type and place instead, for example "a 40-unit housing site in Calgary". You decide what's shown.

We have a lot of projects. Is 5 pages enough?

The $800 site covers up to 5 pages, with your best projects together on one page. A larger site with a page for every project is quoted separately before we start.

Can you add our licences and qualifications?

Yes, on the team page and in a statement of qualifications that clients can download.

Do we need to write all the text?

No. Send us what you have, such as old proposals, your current site and project sheets. We turn it into clear pages, and you approve every word.
