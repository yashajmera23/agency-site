What clients look for on an architect’s site.

  1. Show me your work, big.

    Large photos and a short story for each project. Small thumbnails undersell good buildings.

  2. Have you done my kind of project?

    New homes, renovations, older buildings, shops and offices. Grouping work by type lets a client find theirs in seconds.

  3. What is it like to work with you?

    A short page on how you work, from the first meeting to permits to the build, takes the fear out of the first call.

  4. Can I trust you?

    A "Not secure" warning, or a page that spills off a phone screen, says the opposite of careful design.
